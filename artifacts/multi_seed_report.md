## Interpretation

The two strategies see identical synthetic order flow. The inventory-aware rule shifts its reservation price and may widen as inventory grows; it is expected to trade some fill opportunity for lower inventory risk in particular scenarios. Differences here are conditional on the explicit synthetic generator, the bounded queue proxy, and the one-step directional-pressure assumption. They are not a prediction of production returns.
